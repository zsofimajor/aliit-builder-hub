# The Aliit is Midnight's technical fellowship

<p class="lede">For developers and ecosystem contributors actively strengthening the technical core of Midnight — through code, tooling, documentation, education, support, and leadership.</p>

<p>Midnight is early, complex, and evolving. The Aliit exists to catch issues early, improve clarity, and help other builders move forward. If that kind of work is what you're already doing, you belong here.</p>

## How you contribute

Every member of the Aliit Fellowship contributes in the **Building** lane by default. The two other lanes — **Knowledge Sharing** and **Community Leadership** — are amplifier contributions: they complement Building rather than replace it.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>🛠️ Building</h3>
    <p>Development work, tooling, protocol contributions, and anything living in GitHub. This is the default lane for every Builder and Fellow.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>📚 Knowledge Sharing</h3>
    <p>Talks, content creation, tutorials, workshops, and documentation — making Midnight concepts and tooling easier to understand and adopt. If you're the person who makes ZK proofs feel approachable, or you're representing Midnight at events through original content, this is your lane.</p>
    <p style="font-size: 13px; color: var(--text-dim); margin-top: 8px;">Amplifier contribution for Fellows.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">

    <p>Organizing events, providing ongoing support in developer channels, and helping coordinate the broader builder community.</p>
    <p style="font-size: 13px; color: var(--text-dim); margin-top: 8px;">Amplifier contribution for Fellows.</p>

## 🛤 The Fellowship path

### Builder <span class="tier-duration">~2 months</span>

<div class="card">

**Platform:** Zealy · **Threshold:** 1500 XP (~2 months)

This phase lives in Zealy, with quests designed to surface how you think, what you build, and how you show up for the community. You're not just completing tasks — you're demonstrating fit. Applications to become a Builder are reviewed on a rolling weekly basis; you can apply at any point.

> While this is **not an official Fellowship status**, your contribution should show quality, engagement, and technical depth.

**Requirements**
- Submit original, verifiable work
- Show meaningful Midnight relevance

**Benefits**
- Exclusive Discord access
- Aliit NFT for those who start earning points in Zealy

**Demonstrated by**
- Thoughtful problem-solving and decision-making
- Quality of contributions and originality
- Engagement and support of other developers
- Relevance and alignment with the Midnight ecosystem

We recognize that progress can vary. Builders who have not yet reached the required points after the initial 2-month period remain in the program, but won't be promoted to Fellow status until those requirements are met.

</div>

### Tier 1 Fellow <span class="tier-duration">~4 months</span>

<div class="card">

The entry point for the fellowship proper. If you're accepted into a cohort, this is where you'll land — a four-month commitment where you establish your rhythm of contribution alongside existing Fellows.

**Demonstrated by**
- Consistent contributions without reliance on quests
- Accountability in contribution — measuring impact, being present and engaged

**Expectations**
- Monthly report (via GitHub)
- Attend monthly meetings
- At least 1 Building contribution + 1 amplifier contribution

**Benefits**
- Welcome swag
- Developer tool credits (up to $150)
- NFT upgrade
- Dedicated Discord access
- Community spotlight opportunities
- Fireside speaking slots
- Public speaking coaching
- Conference/course credits (up to $500)

</div>

### Tier 2 Fellow <span class="tier-duration">~6 months</span>

<div class="card">

Members who progress here have demonstrated consistency and are embedded more deeply in the work of the ecosystem.

**Demonstrated by**
- Sustained contribution
- Measurable and growing ecosystem impact
- Strong collaboration with other Fellows and ecosystem members
- Community trust

**Expectations**
- Monthly reporting (via GitHub)
- Ongoing meeting participation
- Continued Building + amplifier contributions

**Benefits**
- NFT and Discord role upgrade
- Private Slack with DevRel, engineers, and product leads
- Monthly 1:1 mentorship sessions
- Official event speaking opportunities
- Eligibility to represent Midnight at conferences
- Priority partner and job access
- Conference/course credits (up to $1,000)

</div>

### Tier 3 Fellow <span class="tier-duration">unlimited · opens 2027</span>

<div class="card">

**Focus:** long-term impact and ecosystem stewardship

- Reserved for contributors demonstrating long-term impact
- Benefits evolve as Midnight lives on mainnet
- $NIGHT rewards, in addition to all existing Tier 1 & 2 benefits

More info coming soon.

## FAQ

<details class="faq">
<summary>Application, eligibility, and general questions</summary>

**Who should apply?**
Anyone actively contributing to the Midnight ecosystem in a technically grounded way — through code, tooling, documentation, education, support, or ecosystem representation. Prior technical experience is mandatory, since this program is for builders. You don't need to be full-time in crypto or employed by a partner organization.

**Do I need prior experience with Midnight to apply?**
Yes. Aliit is designed for contributors already engaging with Midnight in some capacity. If you're brand new, build familiarity first before applying.

**Why does Aliit emphasize Building contributions?**
Because ecosystems are strongest when their technical foundations are strong. Communication and advocacy matter, but they must extend real technical value.

**Can I apply more than once?**
Yes — if you're rejected during application or candidacy, you're welcome to apply again, ideally showing progress in contributions or learnings.

**Can I apply if I'm part of Nightforce?**
Yes. However, Aliit is focused on technical contribution. If your work is primarily awareness-focused or marketing-oriented, [Nightforce](https://midnight.network/nightforce-ambassador-program) is the better fit.

**How is Aliit different from Nightforce?**
Both programs support the growth of the Midnight ecosystem, but focus on different contribution types. Aliit strengthens the technical foundations — building, developer tooling, documentation, technical education, and hands-on work supporting builders. Nightforce is the ambassador program, focused on expanding the community and increasing ecosystem awareness through content, meetups, events, and community support.

**Is there an age requirement?**
Yes — this is an 18+ program.

**Are side projects considered as contribution?**
Yes, as long as they're verifiable, relevant to Midnight, and demonstrate meaningful impact.

**What are the Building vs. amplifier contribution lanes?**
All Builders and Fellows are expected to contribute in Building first — creating code, tools, or products. Knowledge Sharing and Community Leadership complement this by showcasing different skills, and both count toward progression.

**Do I have to participate in every lane?**
For Builders, contributing across at least two lanes is highly suggested to showcase your talents. For Tier 1 and 2 Fellows, 1 Building + 1 amplifier contribution is required each month.

**Can contributions be collaborative?**
Absolutely. Collaboration is encouraged at every level, but individual work must also be identifiable.

**How much time do I need to commit?**
Aliit is designed for contributors who can commit consistently each month — there's no fixed number of hours. How long quests or self-selected contributions take is up to you.

</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Builder phase</summary>

**What is Zealy, and do I need prior experience with it?**
Zealy is the platform for Builder-stage quests and points tracking. Tutorials and support are provided, and it's easy to pick up.

**What if I don't hit the 1500 XP threshold?**
Strong contributions and engagement may allow for a grace period. Evaluation isn't purely numeric, though the two-month period lines up with our cohort timing. If you don't reach 1500 points in 2 months, you remain in the program as a Builder.

**How much time does it take to become a Fellow?**
There's no set timeframe. Based on what we've seen so far, 2 months is a good indication — enough time to build, write, organize, and so on.

**Can I skip the Builder phase?**
No. It's required for all new entrants — this is how we get to know you and how you contribute to the ecosystem. We're happy to support you through it.

</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Tier 1 and Tier 2 Fellows</summary>

**Do I get paid at Tier 1 or Tier 2?**
Not monetary compensation, but Fellows receive developer credits, conference/course credits, swag, and other perks. We may introduce NIGHT rewards for Tier 3 Fellows in the future, once the first cohort reaches that stage.

**How are contributions evaluated?**
Quality, consistency, collaboration, and ecosystem impact. Reports, meetings, and contribution logs help track progress — Zealy XP is no longer counted at this stage.

**Do I need to contribute every month, and does it have to be the same type of contribution?**
Regular participation is expected, and accountability matters for tier advancement. You're not required to contribute the same way each month — feel free to shift focus between lanes based on interest, availability, or ecosystem needs.

**What if I cannot contribute every month?**
Communication is key. Occasional breaks can be accommodated if justified, but ongoing engagement is critical for tier advancement — if you can't contribute every month, your progression to the next level may need to wait.

</details>
