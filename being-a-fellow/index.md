# Being a Fellow

<p class="lede">What changes once you're no longer building toward Fellowship, and are actually living it — how you contribute, what's expected each month, and what backs you up.</p>

## The shift from Builder to Fellow

Zealy stops here. As a Fellow, you report on GitHub instead of earning quest points — your work is tracked through monthly written reports and a monthly check-in call, not a leaderboard.

<div class="callout">
  <span class="callout-label">What this means in practice</span>
  <p>There's no more sprint board to check. Your contribution is measured by what you actually ship and share each month, reviewed holistically — the same spirit as Builder progression, just without Zealy as the scorekeeper.</p>
</div>

## What Fellows contribute

Every Fellow contributes across two lanes each month: **Building** is required, and you choose one **Amplifier** lane.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>🛠️ Building <span class="tier-duration">Required</span></h3>
    <p>Code contributions (PRs, tools, dApps), technical documentation, tutorials with working code, bug fixes or infrastructure improvements.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>📚 Knowledge Sharing</h3>
    <p>Workshops, tutorials, guides, mentoring developers, technical explainers, showcasing projects, ecosystem storytelling.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>🌱 Community Leadership</h3>
    <p>Organizing meetups or events, social threads, technical talks, supporting and onboarding contributors, coordinating initiatives.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p style="color: var(--text-dim); font-size: 14px;">You can change your Amplifier lane over time — consistency and depth in one lane is encouraged, but it's not locked in permanently.</p>

## The monthly contract

This is the core commitment every Fellow makes, every month:

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card"><div class="stat">1</div><div class="stat-label">Building contribution</div></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="stat">1</div><div class="stat-label">Amplifier contribution</div></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="stat">✓</div><div class="stat-label">Monthly written report</div></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="stat">✓</div><div class="stat-label">Attend monthly meetings</div></div>
</div>

<div class="callout">
  <span class="callout-label">Good to know upfront</span>
  <p>Missing two monthly reports or check-in calls in a row without any communication leads to removal from the program. Life happens — a heads-up before you go quiet is really all it takes to avoid that.</p>
</div>

## What "good" looks like

<p style="font-family: var(--display); font-size: 22px; font-weight: 600; color: var(--text); margin: 20px 0;">Build → Share → Help → Repeat</p>

<p>Top Fellows build in public, help and unblock others, take initiative without waiting to be asked, create work that others reuse, and show up consistently. Success isn't about volume — low-effort or surface-level content, and inconsistent participation, don't count toward it.</p>
