<p class="lede">For developers and ecosystem contributors actively strengthening the technical core of Midnight — through code, tooling, documentation, education, support, and leadership.</p>

## How you contribute

Every member of the Aliit Fellowship contributes in the **Building** lane by default. The two other lanes — **Knowledge Sharing** and **Community Leadership** — are amplifier contributions: they complement Building rather than replace it.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>🛠️ Building</h3>
    <p>Development work, tooling, protocol contributions, and anything living in GitHub. The default lane for every Builder and Fellow.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>📚 Knowledge Sharing</h3>
    <p>Talks, content creation, tutorials, workshops, and documentation — making Midnight concepts and tooling easier to understand and adopt.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>🌱 Community Leadership</h3>
    <p>Organizing events, providing ongoing support in developer channels, and helping coordinate the broader builder community.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## The Fellowship path

A structured progression, from your first contribution to long-term ecosystem stewardship. What moves you forward is the quality and consistency of your contribution — not a clock.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <div class="stat-label">Stage 1</div>
    <h3 style="margin-top:4px;">Builder</h3>
    <p style="margin-top:10px;">Quests in Zealy that surface how you think, what you build, and how you show up for the community.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <div class="stat-label">Stage 2</div>
    <h3 style="margin-top:4px;">Tier 1 Fellow</h3>
    <p style="margin-top:10px;">The entry point for the fellowship proper — establish your rhythm of contribution alongside existing Fellows.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <div class="stat-label">Stage 3</div>
    <h3 style="margin-top:4px;">Tier 2 Fellow</h3>
    <p style="margin-top:10px;">Sustained contribution and deeper embedding in the work of the ecosystem.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <div class="stat-label">Stage 4</div>
    <h3 style="margin-top:4px;">Tier 3 Fellow</h3>
    <p style="margin-top:10px;">Long-term impact and ecosystem stewardship, with $NIGHT rewards as Midnight lives on mainnet. Launching 2027.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p style="color: var(--text-dim); font-size: 14px;">Full requirements, benefits, and how each stage is evaluated live in the <a href="{{ '/builder-guide/' | relative_url }}">Builder Guide</a>. Any duration you hear mentioned elsewhere is a rough indication based on past cohorts, never a guarantee — progression depends on the work, not the calendar.</p>

## A note on naming

Across this site, **candidates** are now called **builders**. Same people, same fellowship — the new name reflects what the role has always actually been: building, not waiting to be picked.
