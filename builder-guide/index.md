### First week checklist

- Join the [Discord channel](https://discord.gg/Qm9SnQMTBf)
- Introduce yourself briefly
- Join <a href="https://zealy.io/cw/midnight-aliit/invite/rAd6oibYOADbj59MZv5HV">the Aliit Zealy board</a>, and review the available Zealy quests on [the sprint tab](https://zealy.io/cw/midnight-aliit/questboard/sprints) 
- Identify 2–3 quests that match your strengths
- Begin your first contribution, or submit recent past work

Starting early lets you contribute steadily rather than rushing at the end.

## Purpose of the Builder phase {#purpose}

The Builder phase exists to evaluate readiness for the fellowship. Aliit is built around long-term, meaningful contribution — before someone becomes a Fellow, we want clear signals that they can contribute consistently and constructively to the ecosystem.

During this phase we evaluate:

- Technical ability or domain expertise
- Contribution depth and originality
- Learning mindset and problem-solving approach
- Consistency and follow-through
- Community engagement and collaboration

> This is not a competition between Builders. You are not ranked against others, and there is no advantage to rushing through quests.

The goal is to demonstrate **sustained, thoughtful contribution**.

## Duration and progression {#duration}

<div class="callout">
  <span class="callout-label">Important — how your points are used</span>
 <p>Every quest you complete in the sprint also adds permanently to your cumulative Zealy total — the same total that needs to reach <strong>1500 XP</strong> for Fellowship progression (see the <a href="{{ '/builder-guide/' | relative_url }}">Builder Guide</a>). The 1500 XP figure here specifically describes that month's sprint activity, used to calculate <em>this month's payout</em> — it's a different lens on the same points, not a separate pool. See the <a href="{{ '/builder-bonus-program/' | relative_url }}">Builder Bonus Program</a> page for how the sprint itself works.</p>
</div>

<p style="color: var(--text-dim); font-size: 14px;">Cohorts so far have typically reached this over about two months — but that's an observation, not a deadline or a guarantee. There's no clock running against you; progression is based on your contribution, reviewed holistically once you cross the threshold.</p>

You'll complete quests through the Zealy system and accumulate contribution points toward this threshold. Reaching it signals meaningful engagement with the ecosystem, but the number alone does not determine progression — once it's reached, the Aliit team reviews your candidacy holistically.

Fellow selection happens through a review every two months: our team looks at everyone who's crossed 1500 XP and selects the group moving into the next cohort. There's no fixed date for when the next cohort opens — if you're selected, we'll reach out to you directly. If you're above 1500 XP but don't make the cut that round, nothing changes for you: you stay in the program, keep contributing, and you're automatically back in consideration at the next review.

### How your quests are reviewed

- **Contribution quality** — real effort, originality, and usefulness to the Midnight ecosystem, rather than superficial or repetitive activity
- **Original thinking** — insight beyond existing documentation: new explanations, experiments, or improvements
- **Community engagement** — how you interact with other contributors, including Discord/Forum participation and willingness to help others
- **Ecosystem value** — whether your contributions help other developers or strengthen the Midnight ecosystem
- **Technical depth** — the level of understanding shown in your contributions
- **Diversity in your work** — contributing across multiple lanes, showing well-rounded engagement
- **Follow-through** — completing what you start, with output that's fully usable or understandable by others
- **Community conduct** — communicating respectfully and contributing positively to the culture

Consistency and improvement over time matter too. If you earn 1500 points in 2 months, but after reviewing your quests you aren't invited to become a Fellow, no worries. You remain in the program, and can continue contributing and earning points.

### Submission review guidelines

**Review timeline.** Our team reviews quest submissions weekly, with at least one guaranteed review cycle every week — sometimes more often depending on volume. You don't need to resubmit the same content to a quest. In most cases, expect a review within 5–7 days.

<blockquote>You may not get an email notification for an accepted or failed review — check back regularly on Zealy. We leave feedback under every failed submission so you can improve and get it accepted next time.</blockquote>

Ahead of key milestones — a new cohort starting, major program deadlines — we make sure all pending submissions are reviewed, so no one misses out on progressing due to timing.

**What "reviewed" means.**
- **Success** — your submission is approved and you receive the XP for that quest
- **Fail** — we share suggestions or required changes; you're encouraged to revise and resubmit based on the feedback

## Contribution lanes {#lanes}

Aliit recognizes that contributors strengthen ecosystems in different ways. During the Builder phase, quests fall into three contribution lanes.

<blockquote>Builders must demonstrate activity across <strong>at least two lanes (one of them being Building)</strong> before progressing to Tier 1.</blockquote>

Completing only technical quests is a baseline expectation, not a guarantee, since Fellowship decisions are based on a broader evaluation of contributions and impact. 
</div>

## How the Zealy quest system works {#zealy}

The Builder phase uses Zealy as a structured way to surface meaningful contributions. Each quest represents an activity that strengthens the Midnight ecosystem — some focus on technical work, others on education, advocacy, or community leadership. The goal isn't simply to complete tasks, but to demonstrate how you approach building, teaching, and contributing within the ecosystem.

<blockquote>If you haven't joined Zealy yet, do it <a href="https://zealy.io/cw/midnight-aliit/invite/rAd6oibYOADbj59MZv5HV">via this link</a>.</blockquote>
<div class="callout">
  <span class="callout-label">How the monthly sprint works</span>
  <p>Once you're in Zealy, quests for the current sprint are visible from your own dashboard — there's no separate board link to bookmark or share, since the active sprint changes each month.</p>
</div>

### Submitting to a quest

Every submission should include:

- A direct link to the work (repository, article, video, event page, etc.)
- The date of completion
- A brief description (1–2 sentences) explaining what you did
- Proof of authorship

Submissions without sufficient context may be rejected or returned for clarification. Read the quests carefully - we always detail what we're looking for exactly in your submissions.

**Timeframe.** We primarily look at recent contributions, since the technical landscape evolves quickly — code, tools, and educational content can become outdated. That said, we're flexible for initial submissions: you may submit previously completed work as part of your early contributions.

- Projects, complex code contributions, or organized/attended events: within the past **6 months**
- Content-based contributions (articles, tutorials, videos, workshops): within the past **2 months**

## What makes a strong contribution {#strong-contribution}

Strong contributions usually demonstrate:

- Clear relevance to Midnight
- Original work or meaningful technical effort
- Practical usefulness for other developers
- Evidence of real technical understanding

Examples:

- A pull request improving documentation or SDK functionality
- A dapp built on Midnight, submitted to our awesome-dapps repo
- A tutorial explaining a Midnight concept through working code
- A technical thread breaking down architecture or developer workflows
- A recorded workshop teaching developers how to build with Midnight
- A community event focused on Midnight development

The strongest contributions combine technical depth with clarity.

## Common Builder mistakes {#mistakes}

- Submitting many low-effort contributions
- Optimizing for XP instead of quality
- Rewriting documentation without adding insight
- Waiting until the final weeks to begin contributing
- Ignoring feedback on rejected quests
- Contributing only in one lane

Aliit rewards depth, clarity, and consistency.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Application, eligibility, and general questions

<details class="faq">
<summary>Who should apply?</summary>
<p>Anyone actively contributing to the Midnight ecosystem in a technically grounded way — through code, tooling, documentation, education, support, or ecosystem representation. Prior technical experience is mandatory, since this program is for builders. You don't need to be full-time in crypto or employed by a partner organization.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Do I need prior experience with Midnight to apply?</summary>
<p>Yes. Aliit is designed for contributors already engaging with Midnight in some capacity. If you're brand new, build familiarity first before applying.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Why does Aliit emphasize Building contributions?</summary>
<p>Because ecosystems are strongest when their technical foundations are strong. Communication and advocacy matter, but they must extend real technical value.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I apply more than once?</summary>
<p>Yes — if you're rejected during application or candidacy, you're welcome to apply again, ideally showing progress in contributions or learnings.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I apply if I'm part of Nightforce?</summary>
<p>Yes. However, Aliit is focused on technical contribution. If your work is primarily awareness-focused or marketing-oriented, <a href="https://midnight.network/nightforce-ambassador-program">Nightforce</a> is the better fit.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How is Aliit different from Nightforce?</summary>
<p>Both programs support the growth of the Midnight ecosystem, but focus on different contribution types. Aliit strengthens the technical foundations — building, developer tooling, documentation, technical education, and hands-on work supporting builders. Nightforce is the ambassador program, focused on expanding the community and increasing ecosystem awareness through content, meetups, events, and community support.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Is there an age requirement?</summary>
<p>Yes — this is an 18+ program.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Are side projects considered as contribution?</summary>
<p>Yes, as long as they're verifiable, relevant to Midnight, and demonstrate meaningful impact.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What are the Building vs. amplifier contribution lanes?</summary>
<p>All Builders and Fellows are expected to contribute in Building first — creating code, tools, or products. Knowledge Sharing and Community Leadership complement this by showcasing different skills, and both count toward progression.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Do I have to participate in every lane?</summary>
<p>For Builders, contributing across at least two lanes is highly suggested to showcase your talents. For Tier 1 and 2 Fellows, 1 Building + 1 amplifier contribution is required each month.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can contributions be collaborative?</summary>
<p>Absolutely. Collaboration is encouraged at every level, but individual work must also be identifiable.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How much time do I need to commit?</summary>
<p>Aliit is designed for contributors who can commit consistently each month — there's no fixed number of hours. How long quests or self-selected contributions take is up to you.</p>
</details>

### Builder phase

<details class="faq">
<summary>What is Zealy, and do I need prior experience with it?</summary>
<p>Zealy is the platform for Builder-stage quests and points tracking. Tutorials and support are provided, and it's easy to pick up.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What if I don't hit the 1500 XP threshold?</summary>
<p>Strong contributions and engagement may allow for a grace period. Evaluation isn't purely numeric. If you don't reach 1500 points within the typical window, you remain in the program as a Builder.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>I'm above 1500 XP — what happens next?</summary>
<p>You're now in consideration for the next Fellow cohort. Our team reviews everyone above the threshold every two months and selects the strongest group; if you're chosen, we reach out to you directly. There's no set cohort launch date, so timing varies — and if you're not selected in a given round, you remain a Builder and stay in consideration for the next one.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How long does it take to become a Fellow?</summary>
<p>There's no set timeframe. Based on what we've seen so far, most Builders get there in a couple of months — enough time to build, write, organize, and so on — but it depends entirely on your contribution, not a clock.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I skip the Builder phase?</summary>
<p>No. It's required for all new entrants — this is how we get to know you and how you contribute to the ecosystem. We're happy to support you through it.</p>
</details>

### Tier 1 and Tier 2 Fellows

<details class="faq">
<summary>Do I get paid at Tier 1 or Tier 2?</summary>
<p>Not monetary compensation, but Fellows receive developer credits, conference/course credits, swag, and other perks. We may introduce NIGHT rewards for Tier 3 Fellows in the future, once the first cohort reaches that stage.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How are contributions evaluated?</summary>
<p>Quality, consistency, collaboration, and ecosystem impact. Reports, meetings, and contribution logs help track progress — Zealy XP is no longer counted at this stage.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Do I need to contribute every month, and does it have to be the same type of contribution?</summary>
<p>Regular participation is expected, and accountability matters for tier advancement. You're not required to contribute the same way each month — feel free to shift focus between lanes based on interest, availability, or ecosystem needs.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What if I cannot contribute every month?</summary>
<p>Communication is key. Occasional breaks can be accommodated if justified, but ongoing engagement is critical for tier advancement — if you can't contribute every month, your progression to the next level may need to wait.</p>
</details>

## Final notes {#final-notes}

The Builder phase is designed to be challenging but fair. It rewards contributors who build thoughtfully, learn quickly, and support other developers. If you're already doing this work, the process should feel natural. If not, the Builder phase will help you develop those habits.

Either way, the goal is the same: **strengthening Midnight together.**
