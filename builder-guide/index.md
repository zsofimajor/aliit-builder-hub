### First week checklist

- Join the [Discord channel](https://discord.gg/Qm9SnQMTBf)
- Introduce yourself briefly
- Review the available Zealy quests
- Identify 2–3 quests that match your strengths
- Begin your first contribution, or submit recent past work

Starting early lets you contribute steadily rather than rushing at the end.

## Purpose of the Builder phase {#purpose}

The Builder phase exists to evaluate readiness for the fellowship. Aliit is built around long-term, meaningful contribution — before someone becomes a Fellow, we want clear signals that they can contribute consistently and constructively to the ecosystem.

During this phase we evaluate:

- Technical ability or domain expertise
- Contribution depth and originality
- Learning mindset and problem-solving approach
- Consistency and follow-through
- Community engagement and collaboration

> This is not a competition between Builders. You are not ranked against others, and there is no advantage to rushing through quests.

The goal is to demonstrate **sustained, thoughtful contribution**.

During this time you'll complete quests through the Zealy system and accumulate contribution points. Reaching the threshold signals meaningful engagement with the ecosystem, but the number alone does not determine progression — once it's reached, the Aliit team reviews your candidacy holistically.

### How your quests are reviewed

- **Contribution quality** — real effort, originality, and usefulness to the Midnight ecosystem, rather than superficial or repetitive activity
- **Original thinking** — insight beyond existing documentation: new explanations, experiments, or improvements
- **Community engagement** — how you interact with other contributors, including Discord/Forum participation and willingness to help others
- **Ecosystem value** — whether your contributions help other developers or strengthen the Midnight ecosystem
- **Technical depth** — the level of understanding shown in your contributions
- **Diversity in your work** — contributing across multiple lanes, showing well-rounded engagement
- **Follow-through** — completing what you start, with output that's fully usable or understandable by others
- **Community conduct** — communicating respectfully and contributing positively to the culture

Consistency and improvement over time matter too. In some cases, a short grace period may be granted if progress is strong but the XP threshold hasn't yet been reached.

### Submission review guidelines

**Review timeline.** Our team reviews quest submissions weekly, with at least one guaranteed review cycle every week — sometimes more often depending on volume. You don't need to resubmit the same content to a quest. In most cases, expect a review within 5–7 days.

<blockquote>You may not get an email notification for an accepted or failed review — check back regularly on Zealy. We leave feedback under every failed submission so you can improve and get it accepted next time.</blockquote>

Ahead of key milestones — a new cohort starting, major program deadlines — we make sure all pending submissions are reviewed, so no one misses out on progressing due to timing.

**What "reviewed" means.**
- **Success** — your submission is approved and you receive the XP for that quest
- **Fail** — we share suggestions or required changes; you're encouraged to revise and resubmit based on the feedback

## How the Zealy quest system works {#zealy}

The Builder phase uses Zealy as a structured way to surface meaningful contributions. Each quest represents an activity that strengthens the Midnight ecosystem — some focus on technical work, others on education, advocacy, or community leadership. The goal isn't simply to complete tasks, but to demonstrate how you approach building, teaching, and contributing within the ecosystem.

<blockquote>If you haven't joined Zealy yet, do it <a href="https://zealy.io/cw/midnight-aliit/invite/rAd6oibYOADbj59MZv5HV">via this link</a>.</blockquote>

### Submitting to a quest

Every submission should include:

- A direct link to the work (repository, article, video, event page, etc.)
- The date of completion
- A brief description (1–2 sentences) explaining what you did
- Proof of authorship

Submissions without sufficient context may be rejected or returned for clarification.

**Timeframe.** We primarily look at recent contributions, since the technical landscape evolves quickly — code, tools, and educational content can become outdated. That said, we're flexible for initial submissions: you may submit previously completed work as part of your early contributions.

- Projects, complex code contributions, or organized/attended events: within the past **6 months**
- Content-based contributions (articles, tutorials, videos, workshops): within the past **2 months**

## What makes a strong contribution {#strong-contribution}

Strong contributions usually demonstrate:

- Clear relevance to Midnight
- Original work or meaningful technical effort
- Practical usefulness for other developers
- Evidence of real technical understanding

Examples:

- A pull request improving documentation or SDK functionality
- A tutorial explaining a Midnight concept through working code
- A technical thread breaking down architecture or developer workflows
- A recorded workshop teaching developers how to build with Midnight
- A community event focused on Midnight development

The strongest contributions combine technical depth with clarity.

## Common Builder mistakes {#mistakes}

- Submitting many low-effort contributions
- Optimizing for XP instead of quality
- Rewriting documentation without adding insight
- Waiting until the final weeks to begin contributing
- Ignoring feedback on rejected quests
- Contributing only in one lane

Aliit rewards depth, clarity, and consistency.

## FAQ {#faq}

<details class="faq">
<summary>Application, eligibility, and general questions</summary>

**Who should apply?**
Anyone actively contributing to the Midnight ecosystem in a technically grounded way — through code, tooling, documentation, education, support, or ecosystem representation. Prior technical experience is mandatory, since this program is for builders. You don't need to be full-time in crypto or employed by a partner organization.

**Do I need prior experience with Midnight to apply?**
Yes. Aliit is designed for contributors already engaging with Midnight in some capacity. If you're brand new, build familiarity first before applying.

**Why does Aliit emphasize Building contributions?**
Because ecosystems are strongest when their technical foundations are strong. Communication and advocacy matter, but they must extend real technical value.

**Can I apply more than once?**
Yes — if you're rejected during application or candidacy, you're welcome to apply again, ideally showing progress in contributions or learnings.

**Can I apply if I'm part of Nightforce?**
Yes. However, Aliit is focused on technical contribution. If your work is primarily awareness-focused or marketing-oriented, [Nightforce](https://midnight.network/nightforce-ambassador-program) is the better fit.

**How is Aliit different from Nightforce?**
Both programs support the growth of the Midnight ecosystem, but focus on different contribution types. Aliit strengthens the technical foundations — building, developer tooling, documentation, technical education, and hands-on work supporting builders. Nightforce is the ambassador program, focused on expanding the community and increasing ecosystem awareness through content, meetups, events, and community support.

**Is there an age requirement?**
Yes — this is an 18+ program.

**Are side projects considered as contribution?**
Yes, as long as they're verifiable, relevant to Midnight, and demonstrate meaningful impact.

**What are the Building vs. amplifier contribution lanes?**
All Builders and Fellows are expected to contribute in Building first — creating code, tools, or products. Knowledge Sharing and Community Leadership complement this by showcasing different skills, and both count toward progression.

**Do I have to participate in every lane?**
For Builders, contributing across at least two lanes is highly suggested to showcase your talents. For Tier 1 and 2 Fellows, 1 Building + 1 amplifier contribution is required each month.

**Can contributions be collaborative?**
Absolutely. Collaboration is encouraged at every level, but individual work must also be identifiable.

**How much time do I need to commit?**
Aliit is designed for contributors who can commit consistently each month — there's no fixed number of hours. How long quests or self-selected contributions take is up to you.

</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Builder phase</summary>

**What is Zealy, and do I need prior experience with it?**
Zealy is the platform for Builder-stage quests and points tracking. Tutorials and support are provided, and it's easy to pick up.

**What if I don't hit the 1500 XP threshold?**
Strong contributions and engagement may allow for a grace period. Evaluation isn't purely numeric, though the two-month period lines up with our cohort timing. If you don't reach 1500 points in 2 months, you remain in the program as a Builder.

**How much time does it take to become a Fellow?**
There's no set timeframe. Based on what we've seen so far, 2 months is a good indication — enough time to build, write, organize, and so on.

**Can I skip the Builder phase?**
No. It's required for all new entrants — this is how we get to know you and how you contribute to the ecosystem. We're happy to support you through it.

</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Tier 1 and Tier 2 Fellows</summary>

**Do I get paid at Tier 1 or Tier 2?**
Not monetary compensation, but Fellows receive developer credits, conference/course credits, swag, and other perks. We may introduce NIGHT rewards for Tier 3 Fellows in the future, once the first cohort reaches that stage.

**How are contributions evaluated?**
Quality, consistency, collaboration, and ecosystem impact. Reports, meetings, and contribution logs help track progress — Zealy XP is no longer counted at this stage.

**Do I need to contribute every month, and does it have to be the same type of contribution?**
Regular participation is expected, and accountability matters for tier advancement. You're not required to contribute the same way each month — feel free to shift focus between lanes based on interest, availability, or ecosystem needs.

**What if I cannot contribute every month?**
Communication is key. Occasional breaks can be accommodated if justified, but ongoing engagement is critical for tier advancement — if you can't contribute every month, your progression to the next level may need to wait.

</details>

Midnight does not cover expenses related to quests or community activities. However, on a case-by-case basis we may offer reimbursement. Read the meetup reimbursement policy, and submit a proposal [on this link](https://mpc.midnight.network/aliit-candidate-community-event-proposal).

### Final notes

The Builder phase is designed to be challenging but fair. It rewards contributors who build thoughtfully, learn quickly, and support other developers. If you're already doing this work, the process should feel natural. If not, the Builder phase will help you develop those habits.

Either way, the goal is the same: **strengthening Midnight together.**
