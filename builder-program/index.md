## How the Builder Program works

The Builder Program runs in monthly sprints. During each sprint, you complete Zealy quests, earn XP, and qualify for a share of the monthly NIGHT reward pool.

### 1. Join the sprint

At the beginning of each sprint, we'll ask in Discord what you're planning to work on.

This declaration helps us understand what Builders are working on and encourages collaboration, but it **does not affect eligibility**. You're free to change your plans during the month or even join partway through the sprint.

### 2. Complete quests

Throughout the sprint, complete quests from the Builder Zealy board.

- Each quest type can only be completed once per sprint.
- You're encouraged to complete as many different quests as you'd like.
- Every approved quest awards XP.

### 3. Earn sprint XP

The XP you earn during the sprint determines your eligibility for that month's rewards.

To qualify for a payout, you must earn **at least 500 XP during the current sprint**.

At the same time, every point you earn is also permanently added to your overall Zealy profile, contributing towards Fellowship eligibility.

### 4. Receive your reward

At the end of the sprint:

- Builders with fewer than **500 sprint XP** are not eligible for a payout.
- Builders above the threshold receive a share of the monthly reward pool.
- Rewards are distributed **proportionally** based on each Builder's sprint XP.
- Rewards are paid in **NIGHT**.

Payouts can only be processed after all required legal documentation and KYC verification have been completed.

---

## Program rules

- You must earn **at least 500 XP during the current sprint** to qualify for a payout.
- Only XP earned during the sprint counts toward that month's reward distribution.
- Your lifetime Zealy XP continues to accumulate separately and counts towards Fellowship progression.
- Quest XP values may be adjusted between monthly sprints, but **never during an active sprint**.
- If you believe your sprint XP has been calculated incorrectly, please let us know in Discord and we'll investigate.

## Support & Community

### Office Hours {#office-hours}

We host two Office Hours every month together with the Aliit Fellows.

Join if you'd like to:

- validate an idea before you start building
- get feedback on your implementation approach
- ask technical questions
- unblock yourself if you've hit a roadblock

Between sessions, you're always welcome to ask questions in our Discord community.

### Monthly Showcase

At the end of each sprint, we host a Builder Showcase where everyone who shipped during the month is invited to share what they built.

Presentations are typically **3–5 minutes** and cover:

- what you built
- the problem you solved
- what you learned

We also aim to publish a monthly recap highlighting the work completed across the Builder community.

Attendance is encouraged for anyone receiving rewards that month, but is not mandatory.
