## Eligibility

To qualify for reimbursement, the meetup must:

- Be approved by the Midnight team in advance
- Include clear goals and success metrics (e.g. expected attendees, topic focus, or format)
- Be successfully executed and documented
- Clearly feature Midnight and introduce it to the audience
- Gather names and email addresses from attendees, with their opt-in confirmation for official Midnight comms (e.g. developer newsletter) — this is required of all event organizers

## Approval process

Before organizing the meetup, Builders must submit a proposal [through this form](https://mpc.midnight.network/event-sponsorship-intelligence-submission), which must include key information on the proposed event, such as (but not limited to):

- Location and format of the event
- Estimated budget (maximum $250 USD)
- Expected number of attendees
- Agenda or topic focus
- Key success metrics

The Midnight team will review the proposal and confirm whether the event qualifies for potential reimbursement.

<div class="callout">
  <span class="callout-label">Reimbursement timing</span>
  <p>Reimbursement becomes available after a post-event report confirms the agreed-upon goals were achieved, and the Builder becomes an Aliit Fellow and submits their first monthly report as part of the program.</p>
</div>

<div class="callout">
  <span class="callout-label">Submission requirements</span>
  <p>To receive reimbursement, Fellows must provide:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Proof the event took place, including: registration list with attendee emails, event photos, and the event page or listing</li>
    <li>Approximate attendee count</li>
    <li>Receipts or invoices for eligible expenses</li>
    <li>A short event summary</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Only events that follow the approved plan and meet the agreed metrics will be eligible for reimbursement.</p>
