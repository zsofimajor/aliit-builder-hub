# Aliit Builder Hub

The public site for Midnight's Aliit Fellowship — where Builders come to understand how the program works, get onboarded, and find the resources and incentives available to them.

## What's on this site

- **Overview** — what the Aliit Fellowship is, who it's for, and the path from Builder to Fellow
- **Builder Guide** — onboarding steps, how quests are reviewed, the full fellowship path, and FAQ
- **Builder Bonus Program** — the monthly incentive pool for active Builders, and how it works
- **Being a Fellow** — what changes once you move past the Builder phase, and what's expected of you as a Fellow
- **Resources and Support** — useful links, the event calendar, and the meetup/hackathon reimbursement policy
